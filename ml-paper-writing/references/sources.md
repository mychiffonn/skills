# Sources and provenance

This skill distinguishes observations about exemplar writing, the author's
explicit preferences, and general writing advice. None establishes that a
particular style causes conference acceptance.

## Evidence from 25 main-track oral papers

[oral-writing-study-2026.md](oral-writing-study-2026.md) documents a purposive
sample of five papers each from ICML, ACL, EMNLP, NeurIPS, and ICLR 2026. It
records section architecture, transitions within and across findings, and scoped
contributions. Its paper notes retain identities, examined text versions, and
passage locations; its reading-method section links the official oral sources.
[narrative-structure.md](narrative-structure.md) turns the observations into
drafting principles.

## Evidence from seven additional oral papers

[oral-readability-study.md](oral-readability-study.md) records three NeurIPS
2023 orals, three ICLR 2024 orals, and a separately identified ICML 2024
position-track oral. It links official oral records, exact proceedings PDFs,
passage and figure locators, short text anchors, and counterexamples.
The readings extend terminology and transition advice to skim paths, restrained
heading hierarchy, example-to-definition progression, formal-to-verbal
explanation, and distinct figure roles. Rendered-page checks and text-only
observations are distinguished explicitly.

The two studies are purposive samples, not writing-quality rankings or evidence
that any device causes acceptance. No fixed sentence, heading, or figure count
follows from them. [writing-guide.md](writing-guide.md) contains the retained
principles; evidence notes are optional provenance, not a mandatory drafting step.

## Author preferences

The required rules on internal provenance, early definitions, consistent
terminology, unconventional hyphenation, and repetitive hedging come from the
user's explicit instructions. They are editorial requirements, not findings
about the frequency or quality of writing in the oral corpus. They take priority
when an exemplar uses a disallowed device. The preference for code-generated
numerical macros and `.tex` tables is also an author requirement, intended to
prevent stale values. It remains a concise accuracy principle, not a prescribed
exporter or build architecture.

## Background writing advice

These sources inform general practices; their recommendations are heuristics,
not universal formatting requirements or measured predictors of acceptance:

- [Neel Nanda, Highly Opinionated Advice on How to Write ML Papers](https://www.alignmentforum.org/posts/eJGptPbbFPZGLpjsp/highly-opinionated-advice-on-how-to-write-ml-papers): connect a coherent claim to rigorous evidence and a consequence readers care about; define necessary terms.
- [Sebastian Farquhar, How to Write ML Papers](https://sebastianfarquhar.com/on-research/2024/11/04/how_to_write_ml_papers/): use an abstract and introduction to communicate the contribution, approach, and evidence. Treat sentence counts as a drafting aid.
- [Zachary Lipton, Heuristics for Scientific Writing](https://www.approximatelycorrect.com/2018/01/29/heuristics-technical-scientific-writing-machine-learning-perspective/): remove generic openings and surface decisive evidence rather than withholding it.
- [Gopen and Swan, The Science of Scientific Writing](https://cseweb.ucsd.edu/~swanson/papers/science-of-writing.pdf): consider topic placement, sentence emphasis, and the order in which readers receive context.

## Scope of the retained guidance

The core skill focuses on academic ML writing: clarity, scientific precision,
logical continuity, and readability. Five task-specific workflows support
manuscript work when needed:

- [repo-to-manuscript.md](repo-to-manuscript.md): establish evidence and translate implementation into scientific prose.
- [generated-results.md](generated-results.md): keep numerical macros, tables, and interpretation synchronized with analysis.
- [citation-workflow.md](citation-workflow.md): verify source passages and bibliographic metadata.
- [latex-and-layout.md](latex-and-layout.md): preserve builds, adapt formats, and inspect rendered manuscripts.
- [checklists.md](checklists.md): verify venue requirements and submission-package completeness.

These workflows are not prerequisites for ordinary prose editing. Rebuttal and
review-response procedures are outside the retained scope.

The [figure-design companion](../../figure-design/SKILL.md) owns visual design
when requested. This skill retains the argumentative roles of displays,
caption clarity, and consistency between figures and prose.
