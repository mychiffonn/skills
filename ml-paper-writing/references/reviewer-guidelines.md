# Review the argument and respond to feedback

Read this reference for a manuscript critique, rebuttal, or revision based on
reviews. Consult the target venue's actual review form and policies before
reporting scores, required sections, or disclosure obligations. Scales and
review instructions differ across venues and years.

## Assess what the reader can establish

Examine four practical questions without inventing reviewer-reading statistics:

| Question | Evidence to inspect |
|---|---|
| Is the claim sound? | Comparison, controls, measurement, uncertainty, assumptions, proofs, and scope |
| Is the argument clear? | Definitions, paragraph purposes, section order, local setup, and correspondence between prose and displays |
| Does the finding matter? | The concrete question answered and its consequence for understanding or practice |
| What does this add? | The precise relationship to the closest work, without inflated priority claims |

Separate missing exposition from missing evidence. A clearer sentence cannot
repair an absent control or justify a causal inference. Identify the narrowest
revision that resolves a concern and preserve the author's legitimate choices.

For writing findings, report location, reader difficulty, and a concrete rewrite
or structural change. Apply the required preferences in [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md),
including the ban on internal bookkeeping and the checks for terminology,
unnecessary compounds, and repetitive paragraph-ending qualifications.

## Respond to reviews

Map each substantive concern to a response, supporting evidence, and manuscript
change. Lead with the answer. State what was changed and where a reviewer can
find it, using manuscript sections and figures rather than internal file paths.
Distinguish completed analyses from proposed work. Do not promise experiments
or releases the user has not authorized or that have not been completed.

Acknowledge valid criticism directly. For a misunderstanding, identify the
source of ambiguity and clarify the manuscript. When disagreeing, explain the
scientific reason and evidence without speculating about reviewer motives.
Consolidate repeated concerns while preserving an identifiable answer to each.

Do not inflate novelty through vocabulary changes. Do not add generic caveats
after every result to preempt hypothetical reviewers. Address a material limit
where it changes the inference, and develop broader restrictions in Limitations.

## Recheck the revised manuscript

Ensure the abstract, introduction, results, captions, and conclusion reflect
changes consistently. Reconcile every updated number. Verify new citations,
terminology, and cross-references, then complete
[manuscript-review.md](manuscript-review.md). Keep response drafts separate from
manuscript text unless the user requests their integration.
